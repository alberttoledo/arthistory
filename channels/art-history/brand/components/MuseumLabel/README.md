The plate slide: the whole painting in a gilt frame above a museum wall label.

## Use

- Slide 2 of every carousel. The painting sits in a 2px `gilt` outline offset 14px, with `shadow-frame`.
- The label holds the title in `label-title`, the artist and date and medium in body, and size and collection in mono `text-muted`, right-aligned.
- Use real catalogue data from the museum's own page.
