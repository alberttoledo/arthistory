The text stack used on covers and story slides: kicker, headline with one emphasised word, lede or copy.

## Use

- Order is always kicker → headline → lede (cover) or copy (story slides).
- Cover: `hero` headline, `lede`, a 120px gilt rule and "SWIPE". Story slides: `headline-m` and `copy`, no rule.
- Over a photo, put the block at the bottom over a black shade so text holds 4.5:1.
- Exactly one `<em>` word per headline.

## Content

- Kicker: 2–4 words, uppercase.
- Headline: 9 words max, a hook not a summary.
- Copy: 45 words max.
