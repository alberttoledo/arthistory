# Sources — 06 The School of Athens (Raphael)

## Facts
- Dates 1509–11, fresco, c. 500 × 770 cm; Plato holds *Timaeus*, Aristotle *Ethics*; Leonardo probably as Plato; Michelangelo as Heraclitus; Bramante as Euclid; Raphael self-portrait beside Ptolemy; cartoon in the Pinacoteca Ambrosiana lacks Heraclitus; white-robed figure's identity unknown (Hypatia or Francesco Maria della Rovere often suggested); architecture inspired by Bramante / new St Peter's — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_School_of_Athens
- Vatican Museums: Plato points upward holding *Timaeus*; Heraclitus leaning on a marble block is "a portrait of Michelangelo", who was then painting the nearby Sistine Chapel; Raphael's self-portrait is the figure on the extreme right in a black beret; architecture inspired by Bramante's project for St Peter's — https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/stanze-di-raffaello/stanza-della-segnatura/scuola-di-atene.html
- Web Gallery of Art: "Leonardo is ... probably shown as Plato"; "Bramante is shown as Euclid"; intonaco shows Heraclitus "was the last figure painted when the fresco was completed, in 1511"; Raphael at extreme right with a dark hat beside his friend Sodoma — https://www.wga.hu/html/r/raphael/4stanze/1segnatu/1/athens.html
- Heraclitus/Michelangelo as a later homage, discussed as a much-loved but debated story — https://dornsife.usc.edu/decameronline/2023/08/04/our-stories-day-3/
- Raphael born 1483 (Urbino); age "about 26" at the start of the fresco is an approximation — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphael
- No quote slides used.
