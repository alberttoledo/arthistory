# Sources — 07 Degas, The Rehearsal

## The painting
- Edgar Degas, *The Rehearsal*, c. 1873–1878, oil on canvas, 47.2 × 61.5 cm. Harvard Art Museums/Fogg Museum, Bequest from the Collection of Maurice Wertheim, Class of 1906, 1951.47. https://harvardartmuseums.org/collections/object/303496
  - Harvard: "one of Degas's earliest paintings of dancers"; a grid is visible under the paint; "Degas later reworked this painting, taking out a stairwell and repositioning some of the figures."
  - Catalogue raisonné (MS 220, dated c. 1873): https://www.degas-catalogue.com/en/artworks/la-repetition-220
- IIIF manifest used to check images: https://iiif.harvardartmuseums.org/manifests/object/303496

## Facts by slide
- S3 violinist: visible in the painting; single-violin rehearsal accompaniment also shown in Degas's *The Dancing Class*, c. 1870 (Met 29.100.184/436141).
- S4 petits rats, entry age ~7–8, lower-class origins: Corrinne Chong, review of *Degas à l'Opéra*, Nineteenth-Century Art Worldwide, Autumn 2020. https://www.19thc-artworldwide.org/autumn20/chong-reviews-degas-a-l-opera
- S5 abonnés = wealthy subscribers with backstage access, "preying upon young dancers"; Degas could afford a subscription only in 1885 ("three-day subscription"): Chong 2020 (above). Orsay/NGA exhibition *Degas à l'Opéra* (2019–20).
- S6 studio posing: The Met, *The Dancing Class*, c. 1870 — "Because the artist did not yet have privileges to go backstage at the Paris Opéra, his subjects came to his studio to pose." https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436141 (hedged on slide as "Early on").
- S7 quote: Edmond de Goncourt, *Journal*, 1874 (13 Feb. 1874), after visiting Degas's studio: "a world of pink and white, of female flesh in lawn and gauze" — quoted on Harvard's object page (above).
- S8 1870 eyesight: Met essay "Edgar Degas (1834–1917): Painting and Drawing" (eye trouble surfaced during his National Guard service in the 1870 siege of Paris). https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/edgar-degas-1834-1917-painting-and-drawing
- S8 1874: Degas exhibited at the first Impressionist exhibition (Paris, April 1874) — same Met essay.
- S8 1881: *Little Dancer Aged Fourteen* shown in wax at the Sixth Impressionist Exhibition, with real tutu, slippers, wig of human hair; hostile critics (e.g. Paul Mantz, Le Temps, 1881). NGA 1999.80.28 https://www.nga.gov/collection/art-object-page.110292.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Dancer_of_Fourteen_Years
- S9 Glasgow version: Degas, *The Rehearsal*, c. 1874, Burrell Collection, Glasgow Museums (35.246), with spiral staircase and dancers' legs descending. https://artuk.org/discover/artworks/the-rehearsal-83736
