# Sources: No. 08, The Last Supper

## Facts
- Size 460 × 880 cm; c. 1495–98; tempera on a dry wall prepared with gesso, pitch and mastic; the moment after "one of you will betray me" (John 13); apostles in four groups of three (Bartholomew, James son of Alphaeus, Andrew / Judas, Peter, John / Thomas, James the Greater, Philip / Matthew, Jude Thaddeus, Simon); Peter holds a knife; Judas in shadow clutching a small bag; "betraying the salt" (hedged); Christ's head at the vanishing point; Vasari's "muddle of blots" (1556); 1796 French troops used the refectory as an armoury and stable; Giampietrino copy (c. 1520, Royal Academy) preserves Christ's feet and Judas's spilled salt and guided the 1978–99 restoration; The Da Vinci Code / Mary Magdalene vs John. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Supper_(Leonardo)
- Oil and tempera on a dry wall; deterioration by 1517; door cut c. 1652 "destroying the area in which Jesus's feet were depicted"; French forces used the refectory as stables (1796); 1943 RAF bomb destroyed the refectory roof; painting protected by sandbags, mattresses and pillows; Pinin Brambilla Barcilon's 21-year restoration, finished 28 May 1999. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation-restoration_of_Leonardo_da_Vinci%27s_The_Last_Supper
- Nail hole in Christ's head found during restoration, used for the perspective strings: arte.it, https://www.arte.it/leonardo/loc/ultima-cena-211 ; also https://discoveringdavinci.com/works/paintings/last-supper/composition-and-perspective.html (secondary sources, so hedged as "likely" in the copy)
- Giampietrino and Boltraffio copy, Royal Academy of Arts, c. 1515–20, oil on canvas, 302 × 785 cm: https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/art-artists/work-of-art/the-last-supper and https://artsandculture.google.com/story/IQWBB2AzvHUmKQ
- Head of Judas, red chalk on red prepared paper, c. 1495 or later, RCIN 912547. The catalogue says it "registers mild surprise rather than evil intent." https://col.rct.uk/collection/912547/the-head-of-judas
- Quote: "One of you shall betray me." John 13:21, King James Version.
