# The monkey hiding in La Grande Jatte
# Series: Zoom Into… — one paragraph = one narration beat.

There's a monkey hiding in one of the most famous paintings in the world.

This is A Sunday on La Grande Jatte, by Georges Seurat. He spent two years on it.

Look closer. There are no brushstrokes. Just millions of tiny dots of pure color, and your eye mixes them for you.

Now find the elegant couple on the right. At the woman's feet, on a leash, is a monkey.

In eighteen-eighties Paris, the word for a female monkey, singesse, was also slang for a kept woman. Many historians think that's the joke.

And the woman fishing on the left? To fish, pêcher, sounds almost exactly like to sin, pécher.

So this sunny Sunday in the park might be full of quiet scandal. Now look at it again.
