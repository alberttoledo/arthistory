# Why Orion and Scorpius never meet

Orion and Scorpius are never in the sky at the same time. And the ancient Greeks said that was on purpose.

Orion was the greatest hunter alive, and he knew it. In one version of the myth, he boasted he could kill every beast on Earth.

Gaia, the Earth herself, heard him. So she sent one small creature to stop him. A scorpion.

The hunter who feared nothing was brought down by a single sting.

The gods placed them both among the stars, on opposite sides of the sky. As Scorpius rises in the east, Orion sinks in the west. Still running.

So, Scorpio, remember where your power comes from. You don't need to be the biggest in the room. You just need to be precise.

Send this to the Scorpio who never misses.
