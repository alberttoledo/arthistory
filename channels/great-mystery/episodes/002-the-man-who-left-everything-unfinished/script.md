# The man who left everything unfinished
# Generated from: Three people who never solved the mystery of life and dove into it anyway: Leonardo da Vinci (notebooks, curiosity about everything), Anthony Bourdain (eating with strangers, travelling to meet people), Richard Feynman (the pleasure of finding things out, bongos, safecracking).

Leonardo da Vinci never solved the meaning of life. So he questioned everything.

He left behind more than seven thousand notebook pages. Rivers. Birds. Skulls. Machines. Questions in mirror writing, crowding every margin.

He left paintings undone, too. The Adoration of the Magi. Saint Jerome. Even the Mona Lisa stayed with him for years, never handed over.

His patrons grew impatient. But look closer, at his notes to himself.

One reminds him to describe the tongue of the woodpecker. On another page, he works out why the sky is blue.

He wasn't failing to finish. He was too curious to stop.

For him, the mystery never closed. Every answer opened another page.

So open one today. Write down a question you can't answer. Then go and find out.
