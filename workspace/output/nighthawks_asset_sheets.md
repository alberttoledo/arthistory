# Nighthawks v2 — asset sheets (Nano Banana Pro on Higgsfield)

New version: every sheet is built from the ORIGINAL PAINTING only.
References are in `assets/hopper/painting_refs/` (crops of the painting) plus the full painting `assets/hopper/nighthawks_3000.jpg`.
The earlier shots and face crops are archived in `assets/hopper/old_version/` — do not upload anything from there.

## Settings for every sheet

- Model: **Nano Banana Pro**
- Aspect ratio: **16:9**
- Resolution: **4K** (2K to save credits)
- Upload the reference images **in the order listed** — the prompt refers to them as first / second.
- Generate 2–4 per sheet, pick the best, then check it against the checklist.

## Checklist for every result

- Flat grey background, no floor, no shadows, no text or labels
- Character sheets: the close-ups are the only readable faces; the body-front panel is cropped at the neck
- Clothing, colours and proportions match the painting
- A real, believable person — not a painted face, not a generic model face
- Correct number of panels, same scale across panels
- Save the keeper as `assets/hopper/sheets/<name>.png`
